📝 Ngữ pháp

Will for future predictions

Cấu trúc:

S + will + V

S + won't + V

Will + S + V?

📌 Ví dụ

  • Robots will help people in the future.
  • Robots won't replace all teachers.
  • Will robots do our housework?

💡 Mẫu câu

  • Robots can clean the house.
  • Robots can cook meals.
  • Robots can help old people.
  • Robots will make our lives easier.

🎯 Bài tập

Chọn đáp án đúng:

  1. Robots _____ help people in the future.
    A. will    B. did
  2. My robot can _____ the floor.
    A. cleaning B. clean
  3. _____ robots cook meals?
    A. Will B. Do
  4. Robots won't _____ to school.
    A. goes B. go
  5. Robots are very _____.
    A. intelligent B. lazy

✅ Đáp án

  1. A. will
  2. B. clean
  3. A. Will
  4. B. go
  5. A. intelligent
