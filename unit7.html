🚦 Unit 7: Traffic

📖 1. Vocabulary (Từ vựng)

  • Traffic: giao thông
  • Road: con đường
  • Traffic jam: tắc đường
  • Helmet: mũ bảo hiểm
  • Crossroad: ngã tư
  • Road sign: biển báo giao thông
  • Pedestrian: người đi bộ
  • Pavement: vỉa hè
  • Cycle: đi xe đạp
  • Ride a bike: đi xe đạp
  • Drive: lái xe
  • Passenger: hành khách
  • Seatbelt: dây an toàn
  • Accident: tai nạn
  • Speed limit: giới hạn tốc độ
  • Traffic lights: đèn giao thông

🧠 2. Grammar - It indicating distance

Cấu trúc hỏi khoảng cách:

How far is it from A to B?

Trả lời:

It is + khoảng cách.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • How far is it from your house to school?
  • It is about 2 kilometers.

📖 3. Grammar - Used to

Cấu trúc:

(+) S + used to + V

(-) S + didn’t use to + V

(?) Did + S + use to + V?

Cách dùng:

  • Diễn tả thói quen hoặc trạng thái trong quá khứ.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • I used to walk to school.
  • She didn’t use to wear a helmet.
  • Did you use to live here?

🗣️ 4. Asking and Answering About Traffic

Câu hỏi:

  • How do you go to school?
  • What traffic problems are there in your city?
  • Do you always wear a helmet?

Trả lời:

  • I go to school by bike.
  • There are many traffic jams.
  • Yes, I do.

🚦 5. Traffic Rules

  • Always wear a helmet.
  • Obey traffic lights.
  • Do not run across the road.
  • Walk on the pavement.
  • Do not drive too fast.

🌟 6. Useful Structures

  • How do you go to school?
  • It is dangerous to...
  • You should obey traffic rules.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • It is dangerous to cross the road carelessly.
  • You should wear a seatbelt in the car.
