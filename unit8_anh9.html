📚 Grammar: Relative Clauses

Mệnh đề quan hệ dùng để bổ nghĩa cho danh từ.

  • who: chỉ người
  • which: chỉ vật
  • that: chỉ người hoặc vật

Ví dụ:

  • Da Nang is a city which attracts many tourists.
  • The man who guides us is very friendly.

📝 Useful Expressions

  • I enjoy travelling to new places.
  • Ha Long Bay is a popular tourist attraction.
  • Many tourists visit Vietnam every year.
  • We went sightseeing around the city.

🎯 Practice

  1. The woman _____ is speaking is our guide.
    A. who
    B. which
    C. where
  2. Hue is a city _____ has many historical sites.
    A. who
    B. which
    C. whose
  3. I packed my _____ before the trip.
    A. luggage
    B. tourist
    C. passport office

✅ Answers

  • 1. A
  • 2. B
  • 3. A
