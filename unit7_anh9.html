📚 Grammar: Superlatives

So sánh nhất dùng để chỉ người hoặc vật nổi bật nhất trong một nhóm.

  • The + adj-est
  • The most + adjective

Ví dụ:

  • Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
  • Ha Long Bay is one of the most beautiful natural wonders.

📝 Useful Expressions

  • It is a famous natural wonder.
  • The scenery is spectacular.
  • Many tourists visit it every year.
  • I would like to explore this place.

🎯 Practice

  1. Everest is the ______ mountain in the world.
    A. high
    B. higher
    C. highest
  2. Ha Long Bay is one of the ______ places in Vietnam.
    A. beautiful
    B. most beautiful
    C. more beautiful
  3. Many tourists come to ______ caves.
    A. explore
    B. explored
    C. explores

✅ Answers

  • 1. C
  • 2. B
  • 3. A
