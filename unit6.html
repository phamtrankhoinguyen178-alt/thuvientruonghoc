🏫 Unit 6: A Visit to a School

📖 1. Vocabulary (Từ vựng)

  • School: trường học
  • Classroom: lớp học
  • Library: thư viện
  • Playground: sân chơi
  • Laboratory: phòng thí nghiệm
  • Teacher: giáo viên
  • Student: học sinh
  • Principal: hiệu trưởng
  • Uniform: đồng phục
  • Boarding school: trường nội trú
  • Private school: trường tư thục
  • Equipment: thiết bị
  • Subject: môn học
  • History: lịch sử
  • Science: khoa học
  • Modern: hiện đại

🧠 2. Grammar - Past Simple (Thì quá khứ đơn)

Cách dùng:

  • Diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra trong quá khứ.
  • Thường đi với yesterday, last week, ago...

Cấu trúc:

(+) S + V-ed/V2

(-) S + did not + V

(?) Did + S + V?

📌 Ví dụ:

  • We visited the school yesterday.
  • They studied English last night.
  • Did you go to the library?

📖 3. Prepositions of Place (Giới từ chỉ vị trí)

  • In: trong
  • On: trên
  • Under: dưới
  • Behind: phía sau
  • Next to: bên cạnh
  • Between: ở giữa

📌 Ví dụ:

  • The library is next to the classroom.
  • The bag is under the desk.

🗣️ 4. Talking About School

Câu hỏi:

  • What is your school like?
  • Do you like your school?
  • What subjects do you study?

Trả lời:

  • My school is big and modern.
  • Yes, I do.
  • I study Math, English, and Science.

🌟 5. Useful Structures

  • There is / There are...
  • My school has...
  • I study at...

📌 Ví dụ:

  • There are many classrooms in my school.
  • My school has a large playground.

💡 6. School Rules

  • Wear school uniform.
  • Be polite to teachers.
  • Keep the classroom clean.
  • Do homework regularly.
  • Do not run in the hallways.
