🍔 Unit 5: Food and Drink

📖 1. Vocabulary (Từ vựng)

  • Food: thức ăn
  • Drink: đồ uống
  • Noodles: mì
  • Rice: cơm
  • Bread: bánh mì
  • Beef: thịt bò
  • Chicken: thịt gà
  • Fish: cá
  • Vegetables: rau củ
  • Fruit: trái cây
  • Juice: nước ép
  • Milk: sữa
  • Tea: trà
  • Mineral water: nước khoáng
  • Fast food: đồ ăn nhanh
  • Healthy food: thức ăn lành mạnh

🧠 2. Grammar - Countable and Uncountable Nouns

Danh từ đếm được:

  • apple, banana, egg...

Danh từ không đếm được:

  • rice, water, milk...

📌 Ví dụ:

  • I have two apples.
  • There is some milk in the fridge.

📖 3. Some / Any

Some: dùng trong câu khẳng định.

Any: dùng trong câu phủ định và nghi vấn.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • There is some juice on the table.
  • Do you have any bread?
  • We don’t have any sugar.

🧠 4. How Much / How Many

How much: hỏi số lượng danh từ không đếm được.

How many: hỏi số lượng danh từ đếm được.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • How much water do you drink?
  • How many oranges are there?

🗣️ 5. Talking About Food and Drinks

Câu hỏi:

  • What is your favorite food?
  • Do you like fast food?
  • What do you usually drink?

Trả lời:

  • My favorite food is noodles.
  • Yes, I do.
  • I usually drink milk.

🌟 6. Healthy Eating Habits

  • Eat more vegetables and fruit.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Avoid too much fast food.
  • Have breakfast every day.
  • Eat balanced meals.

📌 7. Useful Structures

  • I would like + noun...
  • My favorite food is...
  • There is / There are...

📌 Ví dụ:

  • I would like some orange juice.
  • There are some apples on the table.
