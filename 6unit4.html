📝 Ngữ pháp

Comparative Adjectives (So sánh hơn)

  • Tính từ ngắn: adjective + er
  • Tính từ dài: more + adjective
small → smaller
big → bigger
beautiful → more beautiful

Cấu trúc

S + be + comparative + than + O

My house is bigger than yours.

💬 Mẫu câu giao tiếp

Where is the market?
→ It's on Tran Hung Dao Street.

How can I get there?
→ Go straight ahead and turn left.

What do you think about your neighbourhood?
→ It's very peaceful and friendly.

📚 Bài đọc mẫu

My neighbourhood is very beautiful. There is a park near my house. There are many shops, restaurants and a market. The people are friendly and helpful. I love living here.

✏️ Bài tập

Điền từ thích hợp:

  1. There is a ______ near my house. (park / teacher)
  2. My school is ______ than yours. (big / bigger)
  3. The people are very ______. (friendly / expensive)
  4. Go straight and turn ______. (left / market)
  5. This street is ______ than that street. (wider / wide)
