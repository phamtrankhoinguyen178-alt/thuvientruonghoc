🚀 Unit 11: Travelling in the Future

📖 1. Vocabulary (Từ vựng)

  • Travel: du lịch
  • Future: tương lai
  • Spaceship: tàu vũ trụ
  • Flying car: ô tô bay
  • Driverless car: xe tự lái
  • Helicopter: trực thăng
  • Electric car: xe điện
  • Hyperloop: tàu siêu tốc
  • Environment-friendly: thân thiện môi trường
  • Convenient: tiện lợi
  • Modern: hiện đại
  • Fast: nhanh
  • Safe: an toàn
  • Traffic jam: tắc đường
  • Pollution: ô nhiễm
  • Technology: công nghệ

🧠 2. Grammar - Will for Future Prediction

Cấu trúc:

(+) S + will + V

(-) S + will not + V

(?) Will + S + V?

Cách dùng:

  • Dùng để dự đoán tương lai.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • People will travel by flying cars.
  • Cars will be safer in the future.
  • Will people live on the Moon?

📖 3. Grammar - Possessive Pronouns

Đại từ sở hữu:

  • Mine
  • Yours
  • His
  • Hers
  • Ours
  • Theirs

📌 Ví dụ:

  • This flying car is mine.
  • The electric bike is theirs.

🗣️ 4. Talking About Future Transport

Câu hỏi:

  • What transport will people use in the future?
  • Will cars fly in the future?
  • Which transport is environment-friendly?

Trả lời:

  • People will use electric cars.
  • Yes, they will.
  • Bicycles and electric cars are environment-friendly.

🌟 5. Advantages of Future Transport

  • Tiết kiệm thời gian.
  • Giảm ô nhiễm môi trường.
  • An toàn hơn.
  • Hiện đại và tiện lợi.
  • Giảm tắc đường.

📌 6. Useful Structures

  • I think people will...
  • Future transport will be...
  • It will help protect the environment.

📌 Ví dụ:

  • I think cars will fly in the future.
  • Electric cars will reduce pollution.

🌍 7. Future Transportation Ideas

  • Tàu siêu tốc.
