📚 Grammar: Modal Verbs (Should / Shouldn't)

"Should" và "Shouldn't" được dùng để đưa ra lời khuyên.

Cấu trúc:

  • S + should + V
  • S + shouldn't + V

Ví dụ:

  • You should exercise every day.
  • Teenagers should eat more vegetables.
  • You shouldn't stay up late.
  • We shouldn't eat too much junk food.

📝 Healthy Habits

  • Eat healthy food every day.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Reduce stress.
  • Spend less time on electronic devices.

🎯 Practice

Choose the correct answer:

  1. You ______ eat more fruit and vegetables.
    A. should
    B. shouldn't
    C. can
  2. Students ______ stay up too late.
    A. should
    B. shouldn't
    C. must
  3. Regular exercise helps improve ______.
    A. pollution
    B. stress
    C. health

✅ Answers

  • 1. A
  • 2. B
  • 3. C

📌 Remember

  • exercise = tập thể dục
  • balanced diet = chế độ ăn cân bằng
  • junk food = đồ ăn nhanh
  • mental health = sức khỏe tinh thần
  • should = nên
  • shouldn't = không nên
  • Để có sức khỏe tốt, cần ăn uống lành mạnh, tập thể dục và ngủ đủ giấc.
