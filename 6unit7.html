📚 Ngữ pháp

Wh- Questions

  • What: Cái gì?
  • Where: Ở đâu?
  • When: Khi nào?
  • Who: Ai?
  • Why: Tại sao?
  • How: Như thế nào?

Ví dụ:

  • What is your favourite TV programme?
  • When is it on?
  • Why do you like it?

📝 Mẫu câu

  • What's your favourite TV programme?
  • My favourite programme is cartoons.
  • How often do you watch TV?
  • I watch TV every day.
  • I like sports programmes.
