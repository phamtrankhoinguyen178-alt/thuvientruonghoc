📚 Grammar: Past Simple Tense

Thì quá khứ đơn được dùng để diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra và kết thúc trong quá khứ.

Cấu trúc:

  • (+) S + V2/ed
  • (-) S + did not + V
  • (?) Did + S + V?

Ví dụ:

  • I visited my grandparents last weekend.
  • They lived in a small village many years ago.
  • Did you watch that documentary yesterday?

📝 Useful Expressions

  • Life was very different in the past.
  • People used to travel by bicycle.
  • My grandparents often tell stories about their childhood.
  • Traditional values are important.

Choose the correct answer:

  2. We ______ a historical museum last month.

    C. visits
  3. ______ you watch TV yesterday?

    C. Did

✅ Answers

  • 1. B
  • 2. B
  • 3. C
