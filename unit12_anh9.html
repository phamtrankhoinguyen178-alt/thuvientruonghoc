📚 Grammar: Future Simple Tense

Thì tương lai đơn dùng để nói về dự định hoặc dự đoán trong tương lai.

Cấu trúc:

  • (+) S + will + V
  • (-) S + will not (won't) + V
  • (?) Will + S + V?

Ví dụ:

  • I will become a doctor in the future.
  • She will study hard to achieve her dream.
  • Will you be a teacher?

📝 Useful Expressions

  • I want to be a doctor.
  • My dream job is an engineer.
  • Good skills are important for a career.
  • We should work hard for our future.

🎯 Practice

  1. I _____ a scientist in the future.
    A. will become
    B. became
    C. becoming
  2. A _____ teaches students at school.
    A. doctor
    B. teacher
    C. engineer
  3. Good communication is an important _____.
    A. job
    B. career
    C. skill

✅ Answers

  • 1. A
  • 2. B
  • 3. C

  • career = nghề nghiệp
