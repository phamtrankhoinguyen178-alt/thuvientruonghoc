🇻🇳 Unit 6: Vietnamese Lifestyle: Then and Now

📖 Vocabulary

Word Meaning

📚 Grammar: Used to

"Used to" được dùng để nói về thói quen hoặc tình trạng đã từng xảy ra trong quá khứ nhưng hiện nay không còn nữa.

Cấu trúc:

  • (+) S + used to + V
  • (-) S + didn't use to + V
  • (?) Did + S + use to + V?

Ví dụ:

  • People used to travel by bicycle.
  • Children used to play traditional games.
  • Did your grandparents use to live in the countryside?

📝 Then and Now

  • People used to write letters, but now they send emails.
  • Families used to spend more time together.
  • Life is more modern today.
  • Technology has changed the way people live and work.
  • Many traditional customs are still preserved.

🎯 Practice

Choose the correct answer:

  1. My grandmother ______ cook meals on a wood stove.
    A. used to
    B. use to
    C. using to
  2. People ______ use smartphones many years ago.
    A. didn't
    B. doesn't
    C. aren't
  3. ______ you use to play traditional games?
    A. Do
    B. Did
    C. Does

✅ Answers

  • 1. A
  • 2. A
  • 3. B

📌 Remember

  • lifestyle = lối sống
  • custom = phong tục
  • heritage = di sản
  • technology = công nghệ
  • used to + V = đã từng
  • Việt Nam ngày nay hiện đại hơn nhưng vẫn giữ nhiều giá trị truyền thống tốt đẹp.
