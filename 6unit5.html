📝 Ngữ pháp

Superlative Adjectives (So sánh nhất)

  • Tính từ ngắn: the + adjective + est
  • Tính từ dài: the most + adjective
high → the highest
long → the longest
beautiful → the most beautiful
Fansipan is the highest mountain in Viet Nam.

💬 Mẫu câu giao tiếp

Which place would you like to visit?
→ I'd like to visit Ha Long Bay.

What is the highest mountain in Viet Nam?
→ Fansipan is the highest mountain.

How can we get there?
→ By bus or by train.

📚 Bài đọc mẫu

Viet Nam has many natural wonders. Ha Long Bay is one of the most beautiful places in the country. Fansipan is the highest mountain in Viet Nam. Many tourists visit these places every year.
