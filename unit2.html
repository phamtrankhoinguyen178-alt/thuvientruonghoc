💪 Unit 2: Healthy Living

📖 1. Vocabulary (Từ vựng)

  • Healthy: khỏe mạnh
  • Health: sức khỏe
  • Exercise: tập thể dục
  • Vegetable: rau củ
  • Fruit: trái cây
  • Fitness: sự khỏe mạnh
  • Breakfast: bữa sáng
  • Sleep: giấc ngủ
  • Cycle: đạp xe
  • Jogging: chạy bộ
  • Allergy: dị ứng
  • Obesity: béo phì
  • Toothache: đau răng
  • Flu: cúm

🧠 2. Grammar - Imperatives (Câu mệnh lệnh)

Dùng để: đưa ra lời khuyên hoặc yêu cầu.

Cấu trúc:

(+) V nguyên mẫu...

(-) Don't + V nguyên mẫu...

📌 Ví dụ:

  • Eat more vegetables.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Don't stay up late.
  • Don't eat too much fast food.

📖 3. Grammar - Simple Present (Hiện tại đơn)

Cách dùng:

  • Diễn tả thói quen hằng ngày.
  • Diễn tả sự thật hiển nhiên.

Cấu trúc:

(+) S + V(s/es)

(-) S + do/does not + V

(?) Do/Does + S + V?

📌 Ví dụ:

  • I exercise every morning.
  • She drinks milk every day.
  • Do you play sports?

🗣️ 4. Asking About Health

Câu hỏi:

  • How are you?
  • What’s wrong with you?
  • Do you have a fever?

Trả lời:

  • I have a headache.
  • I feel tired.
  • Yes, I do.

🌟 5. Tips For Healthy Living

  • Eat healthy food.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Sleep at least 8 hours a day.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Avoid junk food.

📌 6. Useful Structures

  • You should + V...
  • You shouldn’t + V...

📌 Ví dụ:

  • You should eat more fruit.
  • You shouldn’t eat too much candy.
