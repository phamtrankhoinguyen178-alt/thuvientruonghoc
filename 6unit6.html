📚 Ngữ pháp

Should / Shouldn't

Should: nên làm gì.

Shouldn't: không nên làm gì.

  • You should help your parents.
  • You should visit your grandparents.
  • You shouldn't fight.
  • You shouldn't break things.

📝 Mẫu câu

  • What do you do at Tet?
  • I visit my grandparents.
  • We decorate our house.
  • We watch fireworks.
  • Children get lucky money.

🌟 Đoạn văn mẫu

Tet is the most important festival in Vietnam. My family decorates the house and buys flowers. We visit our relatives and friends. Children receive lucky money from adults. I love Tet very much because it is fun and meaningful.
