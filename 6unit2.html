📝 Ngữ pháp

There is / There are

There is + danh từ số ít.

There are + danh từ số nhiều.

There is a sofa in the living room.
(Có một ghế sofa trong phòng khách.)

There are two bedrooms in my house.
(Có hai phòng ngủ trong nhà tôi.)

💬 Mẫu câu

1. Describe your house

My house is big.
There are five rooms in my house.

2. Ask about rooms

How many rooms are there in your house?
There are six rooms in my house.

📚 Bài tập

Điền từ thích hợp:

1. There ___ a kitchen in my house.

2. There ___ three bedrooms.

3. My father is in the ______ room.
